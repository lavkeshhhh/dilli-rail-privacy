A useful, respectful travel companion

By accepting these room terms, you agree to these rules before creating or joining rooms or sending chat. You must be at least 13 and use a nickname you are allowed to use. Keep your invitation code private. Dilli Rail is provided by Lavkesh Singh; contact sutrashort@gmail.com.

What is prohibited

Do not send harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, sexual content, child exploitation material, scams, spam, impersonation, or illegal content. Do not share private information about another person without their permission. Do not misuse location sharing to follow or intimidate anyone.

You control sharing

You choose whether to use rooms and GPS. Joining asks to start GPS and share the estimated metro stop. You can correct a station, pause sharing, block a member, leave a room or stop Live. Other room members can read what you share. Do not post confidential information.

Report and block

Open a member’s card to report or block them, or use Report on a chat message. Blocking hides each of you from the other in that room. Reports are reviewed privately and may lead to message removal, room closure or temporary restrictions. A block is not an emergency or police service. Contact support at sutrashort@gmail.com for an appeal or safety concern.

Travel information has limits

Routes, fares, distance, timing and GPS station estimates are guidance. Some distances and fares are estimates and the app does not have a live train-position feed. Follow operator signs, official fare rules, staff directions and current notices. Use the app safely, away from platform edges and moving traffic.

Availability and privacy

Network interruptions, GPS gaps or server maintenance may delay updates. Rooms and chat are temporary and expire after eight hours. There is no guarantee of continuous availability. The Privacy policy explains collected information, hosting, retention and deletion. These terms do not limit rights you have under applicable law.
