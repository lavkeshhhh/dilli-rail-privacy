network.json is the complete machine-readable app network database.
live-stations.json is its matching native-location catalogue.
station-provenance.json identifies source station nodes and source-snapshot hashes.
All 298 geographic points derive from identified OSM station/stop nodes.
171 distances use connected OSM rail geometry; 7 use attributed operator-published measurements.
146 distances including walks are estimates. All route durations are planning estimates.
Fare/timetable and station-name references are listed within network.json.
No individual user locations, room identifiers, chat, credentials, APK or application source are included.
No warranties; verify current train operation, fares and directions with the operators.
