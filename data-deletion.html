Remove online room data

In Rooms, switch to each room, open Room options and choose Leave room. This deletes your membership, messages and arrival events in that room. A host can choose Close room to delete it for everyone. Finish journey stops sharing but keeps the summary until expiry; it is not immediate deletion.

Stop location and clear your phone

Stop Live or use Stop in its notification. Revoke location in Android Settings if desired. More → About & data sources → Clear saved local data removes pinned stations, favourites, saved routes and companion progress. To remove all local preferences, room credentials and station state, first leave your rooms, then use Android Settings → Apps → Dilli Rail → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall.

If you cannot access the app

All rooms expire within eight hours of creation, with cleanup about once a minute. Email sutrashort@gmail.com with the room code, your nickname and approximate time if you need help; do not email precise GPS coordinates or credentials. We may need to confirm that a request relates to your data before acting. There is no permanent login account.

Reports and response

Abuse reports may remain for up to 30 days to investigate and prevent abuse, even after room data is deleted. Temporary installation restrictions last seven days. You can request a privacy review at sutrashort@gmail.com. We aim to acknowledge support and privacy requests within seven days; this is a support target, not an automated guarantee.
