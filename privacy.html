Who runs Dilli Rail

Dilli Rail is an independent Delhi NCR travel app developed by Lavkesh Singh. It is not affiliated with DMRC, NCRTC, NMRC or other rail operators. For support, privacy requests or abuse concerns, email sutrashort@gmail.com. Effective date: 26 October 2026.

Planning stays on your device

Routes, saved stations, favourites, recent journeys, language, theme and companion progress are stored locally. We have no advertising, analytics, payment or social tracking SDKs and do not sell your data. Android backup is disabled for this app.

Optional device location

When you start Live, the companion or room sharing and agree to the disclosure, Dilli Rail requests Android device location. Android may combine satellite, Wi-Fi, mobile-network and other supported signals according to your device settings and provider. On Android 12 and later we request the platform fused provider where available, with satellite/network fallbacks; older devices use satellite/network providers. We do not read tower IDs, Wi-Fi scans or phone identifiers, and do not send exact coordinates to our room server. The operating-system location provider may process signals under its own settings and policies. Dilli Rail processes the returned coordinates locally into a station estimate; accuracy and station matches are uncertain, especially indoors or underground. Location is optional; you can choose a station manually. Once started while the app is visible, a foreground location service can continue off screen for up to three hours. When notifications are allowed, its notification includes Stop; you can always stop in Live. Dilli Rail does not request background-location permission and does not start location tracking at boot.

What rooms collect and share

Creating or joining an online room sends your chosen nickname, a random installation identifier, room membership credential, and chosen meeting station to our HTTPS service. If sharing is enabled, it also sends your current or manually selected station, how it was selected, and its observation time. Chat sends the text you choose to send. The service stores hashed identifiers and credentials. Anyone with the invitation code can join, up to eight people. Room members see each other’s nicknames, shared station, freshness, arrivals and chat. Share codes only with people you trust. Other members could copy information you share.

Pause, leave and retention

Pausing station sharing hides and clears your shared station on the service when connected; offline clears retry when the connection returns, and rooms still expire within eight hours. The phone may retain your last station for your own use. GPS can continue for your companion or another active room until you stop Live. Rooms expire eight hours after creation. Completed rooms stop sharing and remain readable until expiry. Only the latest 100 chat messages per room are retained; the app displays the latest 50. Leaving deletes your membership, messages and arrival events from that room. Closing a room deletes the room for everyone. Expired rooms are removed by requests or the approximately once-per-minute cleanup. There is no permanent location history. To prevent an invitation code being assigned to a different room, the service permanently keeps a one-way fingerprint of each issued code. This reservation contains no names, stations, messages or member identifiers. Stored room codes are reserved during the upgrade; codes deleted before this safeguard cannot be reconstructed.

Blocking, reports and moderation

Blocking hides the two members from each other’s room map, people list, messages and arrival events in that room. It does not remove them from the group or erase copies they already saw. A report sends the reported nickname, reason, selected message if any, room and member identifiers, and the reporter’s hashed installation identifier to the private moderation queue. It does not include GPS coordinates or station location. Reports are retained for up to 30 days, including after leaving or expiry, to investigate abuse. Moderation may remove a message, close a room or temporarily restrict an installation. Temporary restrictions currently last seven days. To request a review or deletion of a report, contact sutrashort@gmail.com. We may retain an abuse record for its stated period where necessary to protect users.

Hosting and security

The room service runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) in the United States as our infrastructure provider. Data is therefore transferred to that region when you use rooms. Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. SQLite data is restricted to the service account on the VM, and infrastructure volumes are encrypted by OCI. Room credentials are never shown to other members. API and proxy access logging is disabled; IP rate-limit counters are held only in memory until table cleanup or service restart, not in a persistent analytics database. Service failure logs exclude message bodies and invitation credentials. Short-lived private deployment backups are deleted after verification; there is no scheduled room-data archive. These controls do not guarantee absolute security.

App announcements

The app checks a public announcement feed on opening or manual refresh. If you opt in and allow Android notifications, Android may also check in the background, including after a device restart. These requests do not contain your GPS coordinates, room details or an advertising identifier. As with any network request, the hosting service receives connection information such as the requesting IP address. Announcement preferences, cached messages and delivery history are held on your device. Turn announcements off in Your preferences; Android may delay delivery. This does not restart location tracking.

This privacy and support website

This is a separate public information website. It does not connect to your metro rooms or request device location. We have added no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or contact forms. GitHub Pages, the website hosting provider, processes ordinary request and connection information to deliver and protect these pages. If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send; do not send passwords, room credentials or precise GPS coordinates.

External links and device controls

Operator websites, support email and Android sharing open other apps or sites under their own policies. The app does not read contacts or message other people automatically. You can revoke location and notification permissions in Android Settings, stop Live, leave each room, and clear local app storage or uninstall. Clearing device storage alone cannot immediately authenticate a leave request to the server; the room still expires within eight hours. See the data-deletion page for the steps.

Age and updates

This app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. Do not post another person’s personal information without permission. If you believe a child’s data has been shared, contact sutrashort@gmail.com. Policy changes will be dated here and material changes will be disclosed in the app. There is no account or account registration to delete.
